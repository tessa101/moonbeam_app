Reflow rules at AX1+
The sentence wraps freely. A token can break inside, but its icon stays attached to the first word.
Day-stepping ‹ › move to their own full-width row with 60pt targets.
Phase stacks: the glyph sits above the text.
Moonrise and Moonset stop sitting side by side and stack, divided by hairlines.
The dial keeps a fixed size (260pt, widened to the screen). Its N/E/S/W letters are capped at 17pt and use the Large Content Viewer. Only the readout scales.
When the dial is below the fold, a pinned glass bar shows the live heading and "Compass ↓", which jumps to it. The bar turns amber and says "Moonrise · 58° ENE" on lock, so the lock can't be missed.
