What the mocks assume

  • Rise 56° NE, set 304° WNW, passing south. The arc follows the moon's compass bearing along the pass, so on the dial it is a section of a circle.
  • Moon down: the next pass is drawn dim with no glyph. The rise and set markers stay.
  • A, B and C use a 196 pt dial plus the arc, which takes about the same space as today's 220 pt dial.
  • The heading marker moves outside the arc. Inside it, it would sit on the arc whenever you face the moon's path.
