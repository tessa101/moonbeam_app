# Build 5.10a.2 — card skeleton (LOADER.md §12.2 steps 4–5, §12.3)

Updated October 9, 2026. Continued from Codex's uncommitted work.

## Built
- `PlaceCardRegion` owns the card slot. It shows the real card or the skeleton, with a 200 ms opacity cross-fade (none with Reduce Motion).
- The skeleton draws over the replaced card rendered `hidden()`, so it has that card's exact frame. It has the moon outline (breathing 0.4 ↔ 0.7 over 1.6 s, still at 0.55 with Reduce Motion), quiet bars, and "Finding your location…" on the date line.
- 400 ms threshold (`LocationViewModel.placeSkeletonThreshold`, injectable). A fix landing first cancels it, so there's no flash.
- Failure: the skeleton's line reads "Couldn't find your location. Try again, or search for a city." and the moon holds still. The old place doesn't come back.
- No compass and no bottom bar until the real card is in. VoiceOver reads "Finding your location" and moves focus to the card when it lands.

## Beyond the WIP (choices logged in DECISIONS.md)
- **Blocked replacement:** if the prompt is refused or the Location Off dialog shows during "Use my location", that now counts as the failure. Before this, the skeleton would read "Finding your location…" forever.
- **Retry after a failure** shows "Finding" at once. In the WIP, the second attempt left the slot empty, because the threshold timer only starts on a new replacement.
- **No card to size against** (rare): the failure copy shows as a plain card instead.
- Magic numbers named, doc comments added.
