# Build 5.10a.2b — place change: sentence stays, slot held, skeleton minimum (LOADER.md §12.9)

Updated October 9, 2026.

## Causes, against Tessa's video (`design/bugs/place-change-2.md`)
1. **"today" while pending:** with no place, `MadlibFormatter` had no zone for the date token and fell back to plain "today". It now reads the selection in the replaced place's zone.
2. **Sentence blanked and replayed:** the sentence's `ContentLoadIn` was keyed to `loadInGeneration`, which bumps when the old place leaves and again when the new one lands. The sentence now loads in once per screen arrival only.
3. **~100 pt jump:** between the old card leaving and the skeleton appearing, the slot was empty. The compass block (DEBUG buttons) loaded in straight under the sentence. Now the slot holds an invisible copy of the replaced card, and the compass block stays out until the place lands.
4. **Skeleton flash, then blank:** the 0.7 s fix replaced the skeleton ~0.3 s after it appeared, and the card's replay blanked the slot. The skeleton now stays ≥ 350 ms and cross-fades into the card; the card doesn't replay when a skeleton held the slot.

## Tests
- `PlaceSkeletonTests` (+5):
  - date kept while pending and after a failure
  - 350 ms minimum (gated sleep)
  - card replays only without a skeleton
  - replay policy (sentence never replays; card 0, compass +150 ms)
- `PlaceChangeLayoutTests` (new): the real `MadlibSentence`, `PlaceCardRegion` and load-in modifiers in a window, sampled every 16 ms for ~1.3 s through a 700 ms fix (the video's case). Each sample checks:
  - sentence drawn (light-pixel count; an empty strip of background is the control)
  - date token unchanged
  - content below unmoved
  - skeleton shown after ≥ 400 ms and kept ≥ 350 ms
- Full suite **988/988 pass**.

## The hang report
- The previous run's last log line was "building". That `xcodebuild build-for-testing` had finished. The stop came at the next step, the Xcode test run, before any test started.
- At resume there were no `xcodebuild` / `xctest` / app processes on the Mac or in the booted iPhone 17 simulator, so nothing to kill.
- With time limits on, `PlaceChangeLayoutTests` didn't hang. It failed on its pixel check: `UIGraphicsImageRenderer` defaults to extended range (16-bit), so my byte reads found no ink. Fixed with `.standard`, plus the empty-strip control.
- To make sure a limit can end the new tests, the waits are now cancellation-aware: `waitUntil` stops on cancellation, `SleepGate` opens on cancel, and the layout test cancels its fix task.
