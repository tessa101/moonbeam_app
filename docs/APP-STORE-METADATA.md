## Description

Hello Luna tells you when and where to look for the moon, tonight, from where you are.

Most moon apps give you a rise time and leave you guessing. Hello Luna gives you the time and the direction together, so you know when to step outside and which way to face: 1:31 PM, 122° ESE.

WHAT YOU GET
• Moonrise and moonset times, each with a compass direction in degrees and a plain heading
• The moon's phase and how much of it is lit tonight
• A compass that points to where the moon rises, sets, or is right now
• Any city, or your own location
• Step to any day, up to a year ahead or back
• Times shown in the place's own time zone, not your phone's

MADE FOR LOOKING UP
Find a clear view. Hold your phone flat. Follow the compass. Hello Luna is built for the moment you want to catch the moon over the ocean, a skyline, or your own backyard.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account. No ads. No tracking.
• Location is optional. Prefer not to share it? Search for a city instead.
• Moon positions are calculated on your device, so it keeps working without a signal once a place is set.

FOR EVERYONE
• Large text sizes and VoiceOver are supported throughout
• A calm, dark design that's easy on your eyes at night

## Review notes

Hello Luna does not require an account or sign-in, and there is nothing to log in to.

Location is optional. The app asks for location only so it can show moonrise and moonset for where you are and point the compass. To test without it: tap "Don't Allow" on the permission prompt, then "That's okay for now". The app then lets you search for a city instead, and everything works from the city you pick.

The compass uses the phone's heading sensor, so it is best checked on a physical device, away from metal. A "Compass accuracy is low" note may appear until the sensor settles; this is expected.

Moon times, phase, and illumination are calculated on the device. A network connection is only needed to look up a city by name.
